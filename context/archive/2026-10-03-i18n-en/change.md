## Autonomous decisions

1. **Refresh in place, not re-create.** Chosen: keep frontmatter (`status: new`) and the owner's Notes verbatim, append identity + corrections. Rejected: the skill's create path (it errors on "already exists"). Why: the owner wrote the folder before the run. What would change it: none — the run contract names this.
2. **Roadmap: S-16 outside any milestone, Status `ready`.** Chosen: add the At a glance row, `### S-16` block and Backlog Handoff row; leave M-3 `done` and open no M-4. Rejected: reopening M-3 or opening a new milestone. Why: owner decision names only the three rows; i18n is not part of "runda 3 kitu"; prerequisites (S-12…S-15) are all `done`, so `ready` per the roadmap skill vocabulary. What would change it: the owner wanting a language milestone (e.g. more locales) — then `/10x-roadmap` opens M-4 and adopts S-16.
3. **Next step `/10x-research`, not `/10x-plan`.** Why: the `monthNames`-keys conflict with `satisfies KitCopy`, the 13 `format.ts` consumers incl. client-side formatting in two islands, `fillMonthLocative`, and an EN mapping of 405 + `copyExtra` leaves need a file-by-file map before a plan; the orchestrator names research too. What would change it: none at this point.
4. **(A4) `USUŃ` confirm word.** Default for the plan: the dialog shows the current locale's word (EN `DELETE`, own translation, K42) and `/api/auth/delete` accepts the word of **either** locale. Rejected: keep `USUŃ` in EN (untypeable `Ń` on most non-Polish keyboards); server checks only the request locale's word (a cookie switch between render and submit would reject a correct entry). Why: the word is a safety friction, not an identifier — either word proves intent. Mechanics: `DeleteAccountDialog.tsx` imports `DELETE_CONFIRM_WORD` client-side and splits `settings.confirmationHint` on it, `settings.astro` uses it as placeholder, `/api/auth/delete` zod-`refine`s it, smoke posts `USUŃ` 5× — so the word becomes a prop and the EN hint must contain the EN word. What would change it: research finding the word in an RPC/DB check (grep finds none in `supabase/`) (then it stays one word), or the owner wanting a single word.
5. **(A5) No Playwright setup in this slice.** Chosen: unit tests (`resolveLocale`, `format` × 2 locales, kit-contract EN parity, `/api/locale` handler) + smoke steps for the switch (cookie set, `lang="en"`, EN heading, persistence across a second request) + EN visual gate. Rejected: `/10x-e2e-setup` now. Why: design makes E2E conditional on an existing config; setup is a one-time infra change of its own and the smoke already drives the cookie flow without a browser. What would change it: research finding a risk only a browser can show (e.g. client-side formatting in `ImportWizard` after hydration differing from SSR) — then a follow-up `Trigger:` for E2E setup, not scope creep here.
6. **`config-status.ts` banner goes into the dictionary.** Chosen: translate (2 strings). Rejected: leave Polish as operator-only. Why: success criterion "no Polish UI strings except user data" is cheap to meet here. What would change it: the plan finding the banner never renders outside local dev without `.env` — then leaving it is acceptable and recorded.
